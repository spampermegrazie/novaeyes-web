# Pricing: NovaEyes

NovaEyes is a performance-based clipping agency. You pay per 1,000 verified short-form views.

## How it is priced
- Model: cost per 1,000 verified views (CPM), paid from a view pool you fund.
- Minimum campaign: $3,000.
- Your full cost, including the fee for running the campaign, is set out in writing before anything is signed.
- No retainers. Clips that are not approved, or that do not get views, cost nothing.
- Audience targeting: only views from the countries and audience you choose (age, gender) count. Clips that miss your audience are not paid.

## What past campaigns cost
- Past campaigns ran $0.13 to $0.41 per 1,000 views.
- Alex Schor (music): $0.13 per 1,000 views, 189,974,866 views.
- BRAVE CF (combat sports): $0.21 per 1,000 views, 52,131,046 views.
- Sparkle (music): $0.21 per 1,000 views, 4.7M views in 3 days.
- Ahmed Amwell: $0.36 per 1,000 views, 8.4M views on a $3,000 budget.
- Family Ties (podcast): $0.41 per 1,000 views, 7.2M views on a $3,000 budget.

## What is included
- A brief for the whole clipper network, review of every clip, view verification, a live dashboard, weekly reports and a final report.
- Launch within 24 hours of the onboarding call.
