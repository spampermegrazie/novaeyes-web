# Pricing: NovaEyes

NovaEyes is a performance-based clipping agency. Campaigns start at $3,000. You pay per 1,000 verified short-form views from your target audience, plus a management fee for running the campaign.

Updated: 2026-11-17

## How it is priced
- Minimum campaign: $3,000.
- View pool: pays clippers per 1,000 verified views (CPM) on approved clips, from the countries and audience you choose (age, gender).
- Management fee: a percentage of the budget. It is added on top of the budget or taken out of it, depending on the campaign's complexity, deadlines and scale. The percentage is set per campaign and agreed in writing before launch.
- Fee on top: the whole budget funds the view pool, and the fee is added to it.
- Fee taken out: the fee comes out of the budget, and the rest funds the view pool.
- Your full cost is set out in writing before anything is signed.
- No retainers and no monthly commitment.
- Clips that are not approved, that do not get views, or that miss your audience earn nothing from the pool.
- Refunds: budgets are not refundable. If you stop a campaign early, or budget is left at the end, it moves to a new campaign.

## What decides the management fee
- Complexity: formats, platforms, countries, languages, audience targeting and brand rules.
- Deadlines: a fixed launch date or a tight window.
- Scale: the size of the budget, and how many clippers, clips and markets the campaign runs on.

## What the management fee covers
- Strategy and the creative brief.
- Briefing and managing the clipper network.
- Reviewing every clip before it can earn.
- Verifying views and the target audience.
- A live dashboard, weekly reports and a final report.
- Moving budget to what works, every week.

## CPM
- CPM is the cost per 1,000 views. Views = view pool / CPM x 1,000.
- At a $0.20 CPM, a $3,000 view pool buys about 15 million views.
- What moves the CPM: your content, your niche, the platforms, how tight the audience targeting is, and how strict the brand rules are.
